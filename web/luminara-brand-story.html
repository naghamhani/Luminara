77M+ users, AI chatbots, community. Growth over privacy — and they sell reach.

LH-strip scanning, chart-reading. Laser-focused on conception only.

Niche disruptors

Stardust · Ovia · Samphire

Gen-Z aesthetics, employer B2B, neuro-wearables. None own postpartum.

The moat

Why Luminara wins.

  • Whole-person, not one organ: cycle, labs, records, meds and mood live in one thread — everyone else tracks a slice.
  • Privacy without the shallow tax: Euki-grade on-device encryption, but with the depth Euki lacks. You don't trade safety for features.
  • Postpartum is our wedge: a real PPD check-in with a risk model — the one life stage no giant is even looking at.
  • A partner, by consent: PIN-gated co-observation for the weeks when someone else notices the dip first. Nobody else has it.
  • Care that sees you: ancestry-aware, non-diagnostic nudges (vitamin D, thalassemia & sickle-cell, fibroids, gestational diabetes) — health equity built in, not bolted on.
  • Honest by policy: no fake HIPAA badges, no dark patterns, "not a diagnosis" everywhere.
